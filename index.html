Nattis Rules

Overview

6 players, 2 teams of 3 (seated alternately). 54-card deck (52 + Big Joker + Small Joker). 9 cards each, 9 tricks per round.

Card Rankings

  • Big Joker — Highest card, beats everything
  • Small Joker — Beats all non-trump cards, but loses to any trump suit card
  • Within a suit: A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2

Bidding

  • Minimum bid is 5 tricks, maximum is 9
  • Each player bids in turn or passes
  • Highest bidder's team selects the trump suit
  • If no one bids, cards are re-dealt

Playing

  • The dealer (who shuffled) leads the first trick — not the highest bidder
  • The deal rotates one seat each round, so the opening lead rotates too
  • The winner of each trick leads the next one
  • Cannot start a trick with a Joker
  • Must follow the lead suit if you can
  • Jokers can be played anytime, even if you have the lead suit
  • If you can't follow suit, play any card (including trump)
  • Highest card of the lead suit wins, unless trumped
  • Trump cards beat all non-trump cards

Mid-Round Raise

After 5 tricks, the bidder can raise to Little Slam (8) if their team has taken at least 4 tricks, or to Grand Slam (9) if they've taken all 5. Partners can say how many more tricks they can win before the bidder decides.

Scoring

  • Bid met: Points equal to the bid (e.g., bid 7 = +7 pts)
  • Bid failed: Opponents get double the bid points
  • Little Slam (LS): Bid or raise to 8 and take 8 tricks = +15 points
  • Grand Slam (GS): Bid or raise to 9 and take all 9 tricks = +20 points
  • No slam declared: Extra tricks just score the bid (e.g., bid 6, take 8 = +6)

Winning

First team to reach 62 points wins the game.
